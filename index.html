Throughout the day — White & Black Seed Journal (pg 266 HYW)
How this practice works
Pause 6 times through the day — roughly every 2–3 hours, ideally before meals. Each time, write down two things in a small notebook:

1. One white seed — the best thing you did in the last few hours. Something honest, kind, or aligned with your values. It can be tiny: "I listened without interrupting." "I didn't complain when I wanted to."

2. One black seed — the one worst thing. A small lie, a complaint, a moment you weren't your best self. Just one. Be honest, not harsh.

At bedtime: read the whole list. Take joy in the white seeds. For the black ones — no self-punishment. Just make a quiet plan to do better tomorrow morning.

The book says 3× a day is the modern version. The ancient masters did 6×. Stacy assigned 6.
